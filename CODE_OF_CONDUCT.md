# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GithubStudio to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, religion or ability. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback that is specific and about the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect that people have different opinions and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or repeated demands for attention.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers using the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful. Decisions are explained to the person concerned where it is safe to do so.
